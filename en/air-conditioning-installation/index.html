Which type of air conditioning do you need?

An air conditioner moves heat from one place to another. In summer it cools; most modern units are also heat pumps, so in winter they heat using far less electricity than a plug-in electric heater. Which system suits you depends on the space, the look you want and your budget.

  • Split — one indoor unit connected to one outdoor unit. The simplest way to condition a single room.
  • Multi-split — a single outdoor unit feeding several indoor units. Fewer machines on the façade or balcony, and each room controlled separately.
  • Ducted — the unit hides above a false ceiling and air is delivered through discreet grilles. A common choice for new builds and full renovations.

For larger buildings with many zones, we also install VRV/VRF systems.

Sizing matters

An undersized unit never reaches the temperature you want. An oversized one switches on and off constantly, uses more power and handles humidity badly. That is why every proposal starts with a site visit: floor area, ceiling height, sun exposure, insulation, windows, number of people and heat-producing equipment all count.

How an installation works

  1. Site visit — we look at the space and listen to what you need.
  2. Proposal — the recommended equipment and the work to be done, described clearly.
  3. Installation — drilling, refrigerant piping, condensate drainage and electrical connection, carried out by F-Gas certified technicians. We keep piping as discreet as we can and leave the place clean.
  4. Commissioning — we test pressures, drainage and operation in all modes.
  5. Handover — we show you how to use the remote and how to look after the unit.

Renting or owning in Portugal?

If you rent, check your lease and ask the landlord before installing anything. In apartment buildings, outdoor units on façades or balconies may be subject to the building's rules, and in some historic areas of the city to municipal restrictions. We can help you understand what applies before we install.

Tips for using your air conditioner

  • In cooling mode, 24–26 °C is a comfortable setting that avoids thermal shock and wasted energy.
  • Keep doors and windows closed while it runs.
  • Do not point the airflow straight at people.
  • Clean filters regularly, following the manual.
  • Have the unit serviced periodically — see air conditioning maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between split and multi-split?

A split pairs one indoor unit with one outdoor unit. A multi-split uses one outdoor unit for several indoor units, saving exterior space and letting you control each room separately.

How long does installation take?

A simple split is often finished in a day. Multi-split and ducted jobs take longer and depend on the state of the property. We give you an estimated timeline in the proposal, after seeing the site.

Can an air conditioner also heat?

Yes. Most current units are heat pumps and heat efficiently. For hot water or whole-house heating, see our heat pump installation page.

Do I need permission to install in an apartment?

Often the building rules apply to outdoor units on façades or balconies. We help you work out what applies to your case before installing.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the type of system, number of rooms, capacity needed and how complex the installation is. We prefer not to quote generic figures: after a site visit we give you a concrete proposal.
