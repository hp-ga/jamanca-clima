What is a heat pump?

A heat pump takes energy from the outdoor air (or, in other systems, from water or the ground) and moves it where it is needed. Because most of the energy comes from the environment and only part of it is electricity, it typically delivers several times more heat than the electricity it uses. That makes it one of the most efficient ways to heat a home or the water for your shower.

Two main uses

  • Domestic hot water — the heat pump heats your hot-water tank, replacing a gas water heater or an electric immersion tank. In homes with high hot-water use, this is one of the changes that most reduces running costs.
  • Central heating — an air-to-water heat pump heats the water that feeds radiators, fan coil units or underfloor heating. Some models also cool in summer.

Heat pump or boiler?

Gas and oil boilers burn fuel to make heat. A heat pump uses electricity to move heat, which usually makes it more efficient with no local emissions. The best choice depends on your home's insulation, the existing heat-emitter system (radiators, underfloor heating), your consumption and whether you have, or plan to have, solar panels. We assess this on the site visit.

Who it is for

  • Villas, new or being renovated, looking to reduce dependence on gas or oil.
  • Apartments where the equipment can be installed within the building's rules.
  • Businesses and holiday lets with significant hot-water demand.

Tips

  • Leave clear space around the outdoor unit so air can circulate.
  • Keep the outdoor unit free of leaves and debris.
  • Have the system checked annually by a certified technician to keep efficiency high and catch leaks or wear early.

Frequently asked questions

Does a heat pump work well in winter?

Yes. Current heat pumps perform well at low outdoor temperatures, although efficiency drops as it gets colder. Correct sizing and good insulation matter a lot.

Do I need underfloor heating to have a heat pump?

No. Low-temperature radiators, fan coil units or underfloor heating can all be used. Underfloor heating is a very efficient pairing because it runs on lower water temperatures.

Can I install a heat pump in an apartment?

It depends on the space, whether an outdoor unit can be installed and the building rules. We assess each case on the site visit.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the type of system (hot water or central heating) and what already exists in the home. We give an estimated timeline in the proposal after seeing the site.

Are there grants for heat pumps?

There are sometimes support programmes, but conditions and availability change over time. We recommend checking official sources before you decide.
