Why Jamanca Clima

Real certifications. Real team.

In HVAC, the difference is who installs it — not just what gets installed.

Brand-certified installers

Our team is trained and certified directly by Daikin, Mitsubishi Electric, LG, Haier and Haice.

F-Gas Certification — Category I

Legal qualification to handle fluorinated gases, required under EU Regulation 517/2014.

IMPIC-registered company

Construction and installation activity registered with Portugal's public works and construction institute (IMPIC).

European energy compliance

Installations aligned with the Energy Performance of Buildings Directive (EPBD) and sector standards.

Continuous technical training

The team keeps up to date with new equipment generations and installation best practices.

Post-installation support

Warranty, assistance and maintenance plans so the system keeps performing year after year.
