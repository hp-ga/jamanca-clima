Faults we repair

  • Not cooling or not heating — could be dirty filters or coils, a refrigerant leak, failed sensors or a compressor problem.
  • Leaking water indoors — usually a blocked or badly sloped drain, or a dirty drain pan.
  • Ice on the unit — poor airflow, dirty filters or a refrigerant-circuit problem.
  • Noise or vibration — fans, loose fixings or worn components.
  • Bad smell — moisture and residue building up inside the unit.
  • Won't start or switches off on its own — electrical faults, sensors, control board or remote.
  • Error code or flashing light — useful clue, but best interpreted by a technician.

How we work

  1. You contact us — by phone or WhatsApp with the brand and model (usually on the unit's label), a short description of the problem, and ideally a photo or the error code.
  2. On-site diagnosis — we identify the cause.
  3. Proposal before we touch anything — we explain what is wrong, what needs repair and what we recommend. We only go ahead with your approval.
  4. Repair and test — we fix or replace the faulty part and check operation in every mode.
  5. Advice — so the problem does not come back.

Quick checks before you call

  • Check the circuit breaker has not tripped and the remote has working batteries.
  • Make sure the mode and temperature are right (not on fan-only or dry mode).
  • Look at whether the filters are clean.
  • If the unit is dripping, switch it off to avoid damage to ceilings and walls.
  • Note down any error code.

Repair or replace?

Most of the time, repair is the right call. For very old units, serious compressor failure, or equipment using refrigerants that have since been banned, repair may not be worth it. If that is the case we will tell you plainly and, if it makes sense, quote for a new installation.

Frequently asked questions

My AC runs but does not cool. What could be wrong?

The most common causes are dirty filters or coils, a refrigerant leak, failed sensors, or a fan or compressor problem. A diagnosis is needed to tell which.

Why is my air conditioner leaking water?

Usually a blocked or badly sloped condensate drain, or a dirty drain pan. Sometimes ice forming on the unit melts and causes the leak.

What does the error code mean?

It varies by brand and model. Note the code and the brand and send them to us on WhatsApp; it helps us prepare for the visit.

Is it worth repairing an old air conditioner?

It depends on age, the type of fault and the refrigerant used. Often repair makes sense; sometimes replacement is wiser. We tell you our honest view after the diagnosis.
